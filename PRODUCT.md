# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Hungry people in Miami deciding where to eat tonight, mostly on their phones: locals near the Kendall and Wynwood trucks, and people who saw the hibachi burrito on TikTok or Instagram and want to try it. They need to know what's good, what it costs, where the truck is, and whether it's open.

## Product Purpose
Get people to the truck window. The site shows the food, the prices, what other people say, and exactly where to go, then hands off to directions or a phone call. Success is a visitor tapping Get directions or calling ahead.

## Positioning
Japanese teppanyaki cooked to order on a flat-top, served from a food truck, and the Instagram-famous hibachi burrito. Filet mignon, shrimp and chicken plates at food-truck prices, in two neighborhoods.

## Operating Context
- Walk-up window and takeout. No online ordering or delivery yet.
- Kendall: 12750 SW 128th St, Miami, FL 33186, in a shopping plaza lot with other food trucks.
- Wynwood: 1900 NE Miami Ct, Miami, FL.
- Hours: both trucks open nightly, 6 PM to midnight.
- Phone: (305) 522-5141 (call ahead for pickup).
- Busy nights have a line; reviewers report 30 to 40 minute waits.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Menu and prices come from the physical menu board (source/photos/menu-board.jpg). Fried rice comes with egg and carrots. Allergens: milk, eggs, soybean, peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish.
- Open decisions: the Wynwood ZIP code; whether Wynwood shares the Kendall phone number; whether delivery is coming.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Myami Bachi (also written Myamibachi). Handle: @myamibachi on TikTok and Instagram.
- Logo: assets/img/logo.png. The real truck and its neon sign are the brand's most recognizable asset.
- Voice: casual, confident, Miami. Short lines, no corporate filler.

## Evidence on Hand
- Five real Yelp reviews with photos (js/reviews-data.js). Yelp's overall rating is 2.0 from 34 reviews; the site shows only the reviews on hand and must not claim an overall rating.
- Eight TikTok clips (assets/video/social/): three from @myamibachi, five from outside creators (@gabynjohnny, @ronb.insight, @miami.bucketlist, @ozzieratesfood, one unknown). Permission from outside creators is not yet confirmed.
- Real food and truck photos. The hero video, poster and three step photos are AI generated and disclosed in the footer.
- Do not invent: reviews, ratings, awards, press, delivery partners, or hours.

## Product Principles
1. Get them to the window: every section ends one tap from directions or a call.
2. Real food first: real photos and real customer voices over generated imagery.
3. Be honest about the wait, and turn it into a reason to call ahead.
4. Two trucks, one brand: Kendall and Wynwood are always equally findable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text meets WCAG AA contrast over video and neon; motion stops for visitors who ask for reduced motion; everything works by tap and keyboard.
