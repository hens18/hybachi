Yes, there's a line.

Everything is cooked when you order, so busy nights take a while. Call ahead and we'll have it ready: (305) 522-5141.

How long is the wait?

On a quiet night, a few minutes. On a busy weekend it can be 30 to 40 minutes, so calling ahead helps.

Where exactly are the trucks?

Kendall: 12750 SW 128th St, in the shopping plaza lot with the other food trucks. Wynwood: 1900 NE Miami Ct. Look for the purple truck with neon palm trees.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Walk up to the window or call ahead for takeout.

I have a food allergy.

Tell us when you order. Our food may contain milk, eggs, soybean, peanuts, tree nuts, fish and shellfish.
